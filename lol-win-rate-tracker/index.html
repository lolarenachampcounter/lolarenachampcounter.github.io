Your own win rate per champion, game by game

Use this tracker for normal games, or for any personal challenge on Summoner's Rift. Press +W after a win and +L after a loss; it calculates your win rate with each champion and your overall record.

How to use it

  • Search the champion and press +W or +L after each game. Undo (or Ctrl+Z) reverts the last entry.
  • Sort by Most games to see your real champion pool, or by win rate to see where you perform best.
  • Export your progress to keep a backup.

Challenge ideas

  • Win a normal game with every champion.
  • Win with every champion of one role: pick the role in the filter and go for No win yet.
  • Keep your three most played champions above 55%.

Frequently asked questions

Does it read my match history?
No. You log each game by hand; it doesn't connect to your Riot account.

Is it separate from the other trackers?
Yes. Normal games, ARAM Mayhem and Arena each keep their own progress in your browser.

Where is my data stored?
Only in your browser. Details in the privacy policy.
