Privacy Policy

Arena LoL Win Tracker — last updated 22 September 2026

Summary

Arena LoL Win Tracker has no accounts, no logins and no server-side database. Your champion progress never leaves your browser. The sections below describe exactly what is stored and which third-party services the page loads.

What is stored, and where

Your progress is saved in your own browser's local storage, under two keys: lol-wins (the list of champions you have marked as won) and lol-sort (your preferred sort order). This data is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain deletes it permanently. You can back it up at any time with the Export progress button, which generates a JSON file on your own device.

Analytics

This site uses Plausible Analytics to count page views. Plausible is cookieless, does not collect personal data and does not track visitors across sites or devices.

Advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can review and change your preferences in Google Ad Settings, and read how Google handles this data in the Google partner sites policy.

Third-party requests

Loading this page requests champion portraits from Riot Games' Data Dragon CDN and language flag icons from flagcdn.com. As with any web request, those services receive your IP address and browser user agent. The site also links to PayPal for optional donations; no payment data is handled by this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operational purposes as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from them.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by this site, there is nothing for us to access, rectify or erase on your behalf. Your local progress is under your own control and can be deleted at any time from your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the site author, SGiroWeb, through the contact details published on the project's repository.
