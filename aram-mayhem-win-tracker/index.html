Track your ARAM Mayhem win rate per champion

ARAM Mayhem gives you a random champion every game, so after a while it's hard to remember which ones you actually win with. This tracker keeps a simple record: press +W after a win and +L after a loss, and it shows your win rate with each champion and overall.

How to use it

  • Search the champion you just played and press +W or +L. Press Undo (or Ctrl+Z) if you tap the wrong one.
  • Sort by Highest win rate to see your best random rolls, or by Lowest win rate to see which champions to practise.
  • Export your progress regularly to keep a backup or move it to another device.

Challenge ideas

  • Win an ARAM Mayhem game with every champion: filter by No win yet to see who is missing.
  • Reach a 50% win rate with every role: combine the role filter with the win rate sort.
  • Get 10 games with one champion and keep it above 60%.

Frequently asked questions

Does it read my match history?
No. You log each game by hand; it doesn't connect to your Riot account.

Is it separate from the Arena God tracker?
Yes. ARAM Mayhem, normal games and Arena each keep their own progress in your browser.

Where is my data stored?
Only in your browser. Details in the privacy policy.
