Easiest Champions for Arena God

Low-mechanics picks to collect Arena wins faster

Arena God asks you to win an Arena match with 60 different champions. The first wins come easily with the champions you already play; the last ones are where most players get stuck. This guide groups champions that are forgiving in Arena's short, close-range fights, so you can tick off wins without having to master a new kit.

This list is based on how each champion's kit fits Arena, not on win-rate statistics, which change every patch. Check a current build before playing.

The author's picks

These are the champions I use myself when chasing Arena God.

Safe wins

My go-to champions when I just need a win.

  • Sett
  • Veigar
  • Lucian

Stat anvil runs

When I want to build up stats from anvils over the game, these are my choices.

  • Draven
  • Pyke
  • Twisted Fate
  • Jax

Bruisers that heal through fights

Arena rounds are short, close-range brawls. Champions that heal while dealing damage forgive mistakes and win long duels without precise mechanics.

  • Darius
    Simple kit: stack bleed, finish with ultimate. Very strong in extended 2v2 fights.
  • Sett
    Grit turns damage taken into a big shield and his ultimate is easy to land.
  • Mordekaiser
    His ultimate isolates one enemy, turning a 2v2 into a 1v1 you usually win.
  • Olaf
    Gets stronger at low health and ignores crowd control with his ultimate.
  • Warwick
    Constant self-healing and a point-and-click style engage.

Tanks that scale with augments

Health and resistance augments stack well on tanks. They survive long enough for their damage to add up.

  • Malphite
    Armor turns into damage and his ultimate starts fights for you.
  • Cho'Gath
    Grows bigger every round he gets stacks and executes with his ultimate.
  • Sion
    Huge health pool and a second life with his passive.

Zone mages with simple combos

Arena maps are small. Area damage that lingers on the ground hits enemies even when you aim imperfectly.

  • Brand
    His passive spreads burn damage across both enemies in close fights.
  • Zyra
    Plants deal damage on their own and add pressure from a safe distance.
  • Heimerdinger
    Turrets control the small arena and keep damaging while you reposition.
  • Swain
    Drains health from everyone near him during his ultimate.

Picks that shine with a duo partner

If you play with a friend, let them carry while you keep them alive. The win still counts for your champion.

  • Soraka
    Strong healing keeps a carry partner alive through every round.
  • Janna
    Shields and disengage tools that are easy to use reactively.
  • Leona
    Reliable crowd control that sets up kills for your partner.

How to use this list

Open the Arena God tracker, filter by Not won and look for these champions in your remaining list. Play two or three of them per session, mark each win and export your progress after every session.
