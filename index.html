    About Arena LoL Win Tracker

    Arena LoL Win Tracker – Complete the Arena God Challenge Efficiently

    Arena LoL Win Tracker is a free, lightweight tool designed to help League of Legends players track their victories in Arena mode and efficiently complete the Arena God challenge. Mark the champions you’ve won with, monitor your remaining picks, and organize your progress in seconds.

    What Is the Arena God Challenge?

    Arena God is a League of Legends challenge that asks players to win at least one Arena match with every champion in the game — currently over 160. Riot doesn't provide an in-client checklist for this, so most players end up guessing which champions they've already used. This tracker replaces the guesswork with a simple, visual list you can filter and search as you play.

    Why Use This Tracker?

    Completing the Arena God challenge requires winning with 160+ champions. Without tracking, it’s easy to forget who you’ve already used. This tool helps you:

    • Identify champions you still need to win with
    • Avoid repeating already completed champions
    • Plan your next Arena sessions strategically
    • Track progress clearly and efficiently

    Tips for Finishing Arena God Faster

    • Clear low-pick-rate champions first. Champions you rarely play (or that feel weak in Arena) are the ones most likely to get postponed indefinitely — sort by "Not won first" and knock them out early while you still have motivation.
    • Pick augments around your champion's win condition, not your habits. Arena's augment pool can turn a normally weak pick into a duo-carry build; leaning into whatever the augments offer usually wins more than forcing your main's normal playstyle.
    • Play duos with a partner who covers your weaknesses. A champion with poor early game pairs well with a duo who can peel or engage for you in the opening rounds, when Arena games are most often decided.
    • Track progress across sessions, not single games. Export your progress after each session so a browser cache clear, device switch, or reinstall never costs you hours of tracked wins.

    Core Features

    • ✔ Mark victories for each champion
    • ✔ Filter by won or not-won champions
    • ✔ Instant champion search
    • ✔ Sort alphabetically or by win status
    • ✔ Export and import progress (JSON format)
    • ✔ Multi-language support (English & Spanish)
    • ✔ Fully responsive for desktop and mobile

    Yes, it's completely free, with no account required and no personal data collected.

    Yes, export your progress as a JSON file and import it on any device.

    How many champions do I need for Arena God?
    You need at least one Arena win with every champion currently in the game (160+).

    Privacy & Data

    Your progress is stored locally in your browser. No accounts, no logins, and no personal data collection. You can export your progress at any time to back it up or move it to another device.

    Whether you're a casual player aiming to complete the challenge gradually or a competitive player optimizing every session, this Arena LoL Tracker helps you stay organized and focused. Save time, track smarter, and reach the Arena God title faster.
